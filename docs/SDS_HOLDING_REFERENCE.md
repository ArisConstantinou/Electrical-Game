Implementation criteria: fixed chosen dominant hand; right is default. Lateral blade angle must not exchange hands. Bounded clavicle movement, an elbow crease aligned to the actual bend plane, neutral wrist and both physical grip contacts. Oblique work seats the torso over the shared tool support and braces it with wider, staggered planted feet. Body yaw follows the player's aim; independently adding tool-angle yaw was rejected because it moved one shoulder outside its grip's reach. Blade contact and real masonry impacts remain required. A static contact image cannot establish transition continuity or whole-game performance.

Photos were viewed as references and linked to their sources. They were not copied into game assets or the public screenshot gallery. Our published gallery contains actual runtime captures only.
